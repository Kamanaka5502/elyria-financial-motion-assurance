# Product

## Elyria Motion Guard

Elyria Motion Guard is an old-stack compatible pre-execution motion-admission wrapper for AI-assisted financial intent.

It is the small sellable corridor around the larger Elyria / Veritas motion-governance substrate.

## Buyer Problem

AI-assisted finance workflows can produce intent faster than old-stack controls can safely evaluate consequence.

A model can recommend a payment.
A workflow can approve a transfer.
A vendor can be generated.
A log can exist.

The financial motion may still lack standing to bind.

## Product Answer

Motion Guard checks whether payment intent may become value-bearing motion before consequence binds.

```text
payment intent packet
  -> invariant checks
  -> admission / non-admission outcome
  -> replayable receipt
```

## Target Buyers

```text
fractional CFOs
bookkeeping firms
accounts payable teams
small finance operations
AI automation agencies
vendor-payment workflows
payment operations consultants
```

## Wrapper Value

Motion Guard does not ask buyers to adopt the full protected substrate.

It gives them a simple old-stack interface:

```text
JSON in
Decision out
Receipt attached
Replay available
```

## Commercial Offer Shape

```text
Starter setup: $500–$1,500
Configured workflow: $2,500–$7,500
Bounded pilot: $10,000–$25,000
Enterprise substrate: separate private engagement
```

## Non-Claims

Motion Guard is not a bank platform, payment processor, custody service, regulatory certification, compliance substitute, or payment execution tool.

It is a pre-execution motion-admission wrapper.
